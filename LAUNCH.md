# Sharing Planet Parade: the path from "my dad built this" to a game other families can open

*2026-10-09. The audience is families: nieces and nephews, a LinkedIn post, a link sent to a friend.
The school board has a different app, built with the dev team, with its own research passes.*

## The bar
A child opens the link with no grown-up setup and is playing something at their level within a
minute. That bar is now met by the first-launch sheet (age band plus optional name, one tap).

## The asset
Nothing leaves the device: no accounts, no tracking, no network calls during play, offline as one
file, `PRIVACY.md` says "collects nothing". That is the one line other parents care about. Keep it.

## Before the post
1. **Record a real child playing**, 30 seconds, phone held over the tablet. Lead with that, not
   the link. Adults believe a kid's face before they believe a demo.
2. **Voice.** The game uses the device's voices. On an iPad or a Windows laptop with a natural
   voice it sounds good; on a bare laptop it can sound robotic, and Farsi voices are often missing
   (captions still show). Say so in the post, in one line.
3. **French reviewed by a person.** All French and Farsi lines are still marked unreviewed. Family
   first, then anyone else.
4. **A licence line.** The repo says "all rights reserved for now" and the app is free to use.
   Decide what people may do with it before it spreads.

## What the game does for engagement (and what it does not pretend to)
- Short visits, a map that remembers, a moon for every mastered step, a fact spoken with each new
  moon, the Playground games as the open-ended hook.
- The ramp inside every level keeps a strong child challenged; "pending" lets a strong day move
  forward; the two-day rule keeps moons honest.
- It never says "wrong", never shows a score, never praises the person. After a second miss it shows
  what is right and counts together.
- It is quiet socially. The grown-up's part is the Today panel and, next, a co-play prompt per visit.

## Content growth, in evidence order (see `content/curriculum/EVIDENCE.md`)
1. The 11 planned levels: language (authored natively per language), tangram, living needs.
2. Co-play prompt in the Today panel (one line per level played).
3. Stretch variants at the top of Number: compose a number several ways, estimate on 0–100, debug a
   sequence.
4. Science beyond space as `observe_change` items with sourced facts; interactive sims (a Godot HTML5
   export in a window) fit this better than generated video, which cannot carry a source.
5. Coding precursors stay at sequencing and fix-the-path until 5; rover `debug` mode is the shape.

## What not to do
No accounts, analytics, leaderboards, streaks, stickers or "gifted" labels. No age gates: the engine
gates on demonstrated mastery. No AI-generated science footage presented as fact.
