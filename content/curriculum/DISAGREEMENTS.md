| # | Topic | Positions | Choice | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Music/Rhythm strand | Astra: optional, deferred. Fable: 8 levels. Gemini: 10 levels. | Included, 10 levels at Uranus, **built last**. | Two sources support it, which meets the inclusion bar. Astra's caution sets the priority. Gemini's loop-builder level is dropped because it is creative and unassessable. |
| 2 | Mastery threshold | Astra: 4/5 in each of 2 sessions, plus transfer and 2 representations. Fable: 4/5 × 2 visits, with 5/6 and 3/4 variants. Gemini: 5/5 on some levels. | 3 | Placement probe | Gemini: probe L, L+2, L+4 and jump bands. Fable: 3 items at mid-band, ±1. Astra: prerequisite + target + transfer; a probe is not mastery. Existing `applyProbe` marks skipped levels done. | Astra's probe. Skipped levels are "placed", not "done". | Gemini's jump can skip a prerequisite for a raise, and Kimi's guardrail forbids that. | 4 | Step-back | Existing code: permanent level−1. Fable: drop for this visit only. Gemini: after struggle across 2 sessions. Direct comparison is safe at 2 in every draft. |
